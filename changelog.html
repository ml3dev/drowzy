What's New
Version 1.3.0
New Features
  • Close Duplicate Tabs
    One-click button to find and close duplicate tabs in the current window. Keeps the first occurrence and closes the rest.
  • Tab Search
    Search and filter your tab list by title or URL. Great for quickly finding a specific tab in a crowded window.
  • Dark / Light Theme
    Toggle between dark and light mode with the new theme button in the header. Your preference is saved across sessions.
  • Side Panel
    Open Drowzy in Chrome's side panel for a full-height view that stays open while you browse. Same interface, more room.
  • Mark Suspended Tabs
    Optional setting to prefix suspended tab titles with [zzz], making them easy to spot in your tab bar at a glance.
Improvements
  • 57 Languages
    Drowzy is now fully translated into 57 languages. The UI automatically matches your browser's language setting.
  • Export Tab List
    Copy your full tab list (titles and URLs) to the clipboard with one click. Handy for sharing or saving a snapshot.
  • Export Sessions as JSON
    Copy all saved sessions to your clipboard as JSON for backup or transfer to another machine.
  • Suspend Warning
    Tabs now show a brief "Suspending tab soon..." notice before being auto-suspended, so you're never caught off guard.

Drowzy is free and open source. If it's saving you memory, consider leaving a quick review or starring us on GitHub.
