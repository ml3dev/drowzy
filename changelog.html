What's New
What's new in 1.5.0September 2026
  • Opens instantly, even with 100 tabs
    The popup no longer keeps you waiting. It opens right away, however many tabs you have open.
  • Suspend Others is faster and shows its work
    It puts tabs to sleep several at a time, and you can watch the count climb. If some tabs could not be put to sleep, it tells you how many instead of claiming it worked.
  • A 1 minute timer
    Opened a pile of tabs from a search? Set the timer to 1 minute and they go to sleep soon after you leave them.
  • Shortcuts are easy to change now
    Want a different key for Suspend this tab, or none at all? The Keyboard shortcuts row in Settings now takes you straight to the right place.
  • And the small stuff
    Paste a full web address into the whitelist and it just works. Suspend this tab will not move you off a page you are typing on. Messages are clearer and never get cut off.
What's new in 1.4.0August 2026
  • Keep this tab awake
    Hold a single tab awake without whitelisting the whole site. Drowzy won't sleep it until you press Allow sleep or close the tab. The hold clears when you restart your browser.
  • Protect audio tabs now works both ways
    Turning off Protect audio tabs really does let background video and music tabs sleep now. Before, they were refused even with the setting off. With it on, nothing changes.
  • Drowzy tells you why a tab won't sleep
    Suspend this tab no longer disappears without explanation. When a tab can't sleep, you get the reason, and a shortcut straight to the setting that would change it.
  • Site icons that actually show up
    Tab icons now come from your browser's own cache, so sites that used to fall back to a grey letter show their real icon, including while they are asleep. Icons that would vanish against the theme you are using are adjusted so you can still see them.
  • Calmer, and counts you can trust
    Suspending tabs no longer makes the whole list bounce. This page and the welcome page now follow the light or dark theme you picked in Drowzy. And re-sleeping the same tab over and over no longer inflates your lifetime stats.
What's new in 1.3.8July 2026
  • Restored tabs go back to sleep sooner
    After a browser restart, Drowzy now keeps watching for tabs your browser restores late and puts them back to sleep within about a minute. Tabs you've viewed are left alone.
  • Report a bug from Settings
    A new Report a bug link in Settings opens Drowzy's public issue tracker in a new tab. Nothing is ever sent automatically.
  • Small clarity touches
    Sleeping tabs now show a click-to-wake hint, and the welcome page explains how Drowzy works alongside your browser's built-in Memory Saver.
What's new in 1.3.7June 2026
  • Put a single tab back to sleep
    After you wake a sleeping tab to look at it, the new Suspend this tab button in the popup and side panel sends just that tab back to sleep. It switches you to another tab first, because the tab you're viewing can't be suspended.
What's new in 1.3.6May 2026
  • Every language is now fully translated
    A few strings (whitelist toasts, the Keyboard Shortcuts row, and the Share Stats text) were still showing in English in other languages. They are now translated across all 57 languages, and right-to-left languages like Arabic and Hebrew lay out correctly too.
  • A gentler review prompt
    The "Enjoying Drowzy?" banner now appears only after you've used Drowzy for a while, never on install or right after an update. The buttons are Leave a review, Maybe later, and Don't ask again. It never asks for a specific star rating and never offers a reward.
What's new in 1.3.5May 2026
  • Faster, smarter suspending
    The default timer is now 15 minutes so you notice the memory savings sooner, and suspending other tabs and closing duplicates are more reliable.
  • Whitelist is easier to manage
    Clearer remove buttons, confirmation messages, and a site count, plus it now works for localhost and IP addresses.
  • Keyboard shortcuts in Settings
    A new Settings row shows your shortcut status and links straight to where you can customize them.
  • Stats and sharing improvements
    Share Stats now appears in your language, and memory totals read cleanly, for example 2 GB instead of 2.0 GB.
  • Polish and accessibility
    Clearer icons, a brand-matched toolbar badge, better screen-reader labels, and a tidier popup.
