Drowzy - Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 2026

The short version

Drowzy doesn't collect any data. No analytics, no tracking, no external servers. Everything stays in your browser.

What gets stored

Your settings (suspend timer, whitelist, preferences) are saved using Chrome's built-in storage APIs: chrome.storage.sync for settings that follow your Chrome profile, chrome.storage.local for stats and saved sessions, and chrome.storage.session for temporary data like tab timestamps. None of this is sent to any third-party server.

Permissions

Drowzy uses these Chrome permissions:

  • tabs – listing, suspending, and restoring browser tabs
  • storage – saving your settings locally
  • alarms – periodic checks for inactive tabs
  • contextMenus – right-click menu options
