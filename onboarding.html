Drowzy

Drowzy saves memory by putting inactive tabs to sleep. Your tabs are always safe -- we use Chrome's native discard API, so they survive even if you uninstall Drowzy.

How it works

  • Tabs idle for 30 minutes are automatically suspended
  • Suspended tabs use zero RAM but stay in your tab bar
  • Click any suspended tab to instantly wake it up
  • Active, pinned, and audio tabs are never touched

Quick actions

  • •Click the Drowzy icon in the toolbar to see all your tabs
  • •Press Alt+S to suspend the current tab
  • •Press Alt+Shift+S to suspend all other tabs
  • •Press Alt+W to wake all suspended tabs
  • •Right-click on any page for quick suspend options

Tip: Click the puzzle icon in your Chrome toolbar to pin Drowzy for one-click access.

Suspending tabs you still want? Open Drowzy → Settings to bump the 30-minute timer up, or add the site to your whitelist with one click.

Filling out a form? Enable "Protect tabs with forms" in Settings to keep tabs with unsaved input from being suspended.

Drowzy is now running. Open it from your toolbar and hit "Suspend Others" to try it out.
