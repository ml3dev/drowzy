Why Drowzy

Most suspenders gamble with your tabs.

They swap each tab for a placeholder page of their own. If that extension breaks, gets pulled from the store, or you remove it, those tabs can disappear right along with it.

Instead, Drowzy uses the chrome.tabs.discard() API that Chrome relies on to free memory itself.

  • Chrome owns the sleeping tab

    Drowzy hands suspension to Chrome's own engine, so a tab survives restarts, crashes, and even removing the extension.

  • Everything stays intact

    Full history, scroll position, and anything typed into a form is exactly where you left it when the tab wakes.

  • No placeholder pages

    No redirects, no fake "this tab is suspended" screens, and no chrome-extension URLs cluttering your tab bar.

  • Instant to wake

    Click the tab and Chrome reloads it on the spot. No extra step, no in-between page to click through.

The whole point

Private by design

Drowzy never sends your data anywhere.

Drowzy has no servers, no accounts, and no third-party tracking. Your settings, sessions, and stats are all counted and kept on your own machine, and none of it is ever uploaded anywhere, not even to us. The stats you see are simply your own numbers, shown back to you.

Tab suspenders have a history of being quietly sold and turned into trackers. Drowzy is built so that cannot happen in the dark: the code is public, and there is no server for it to send anything to.
